## Overview

Integrating GitHub with Softgen is straightforward. By providing your GitHub username, Softgen can add you as a collaborator to the repository, allowing seamless access and collaboration.

## 🔗 How to Connect Your GitHub Account & Access the Repository
 
1. Go to your **Project Dashboard**  
2. Open the **Project Name** dropdown  
3. Click on **GitHub Repository**  
4. Invite yourself as a **collaborator** to the repository  
5. You’ll receive an email invitation, accept it to clone the project and collaborate as needed  

> **Note:** If the invite doesn’t show up, please check your spam or promotions folder. Feel free to reach out to our support team if you need help.

## Benefits

- **Easy Access**: No need for complex setup; just your username
- **Collaboration**: Work directly on the repository with full access
- **Version Control**: Track changes and manage your project efficiently
