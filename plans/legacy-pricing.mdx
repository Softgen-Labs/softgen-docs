If you're a long-time Softgen user, you may have noticed changes to our pricing model. This page explains what changed, why we made the switch, and how it benefits you.

## What Changed?

On **August 14, 2025**, we transitioned from monthly token-based subscription plans to a new **Annual Membership + Pay-as-You-Go** model.

### Old Pricing Model (Before August 2025)

Our previous pricing structure was based on monthly subscriptions with allocated tokens:

| Plan | Monthly Cost | Tokens Included |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| **Free** | $0/month | 50K tokens |
| **Entry** | $25/month | 2.5M tokens per month |
| **Boost** | $50/month | 6M tokens per month |
| **Fly** | $100/month | Unlimited tokens |

**Key characteristics:**
- Monthly recurring charges
- Fixed token allocations per month
- Tokens did not roll over month-to-month
- If you didn't use your tokens, you lost them

### New Pricing Model (After August 2025)

Our current pricing is designed to be simpler and more cost-effective:

| Component | Cost | What You Get |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| **Annual Membership** | $33/year | Platform access + lowest pricing |
| **Wallet (Pay-as-You-Go)** | Variable | Pay only for what you use |

**Key characteristics:**
- Low annual membership fee ($33/year = $2.75/month)
- Transparent platform fee (currently 15%, decreases as community grows)
- Only pay for actual usage via wallet credits
- No wasted tokens - use what you need, when you need it
- Credits don't expire

## Why Did We Make This Change?

### 1. **Better Value for Users**

The old model forced you to pay for tokens you might not use. With the new model:
- You only pay for what you actually use
- Our 15% platform fee is significantly lower than industry standards
- Most users save 60-90% compared to the old plans

### 2. **Transparency**

- Clear breakdown of costs before every payment
- Real-time transaction history
- No surprises or hidden fees
- Full visibility into platform fees

### 3. **Fairness**

- Heavy users aren't subsidizing light users
- Community benefits: platform fees decrease as membership grows
- Referral program lets you earn credits

### 4. **Flexibility**

- No pressure to "use it or lose it"
- Add funds whenever you need them
- Scale usage up or down based on your projects

## Cost Comparison Example

Let's compare the old **Entry plan** ($25/month) with the new model:

### Old Model (Entry Plan)
- **Annual cost:** $300/year ($25 × 12 months)
- **Tokens:** 2.5M per month (30M total/year)
- **Catch:** Unused tokens disappeared each month

### New Model
- **Membership:** $33/year (one-time annual fee)
- **Usage:** Pay only for what you use
- **Platform fee:** 15% markup (decreases with community growth)
- **Result:** Most users spend 60-90% less overall


## Common Questions from Legacy Users

### "I never agreed to this new pricing!"

You're absolutely right - and that's not what happened. If you were on a legacy plan (Free, Entry, Boost, or Fly), **you remained on that legacy plan** until you chose to switch or cancel.

What likely happened:
1. You were on the old monthly plan
2. You may not have logged in for several months
3. When you logged back in, you saw the new pricing page
4. This created confusion - but you were still being charged the old rate

We understand this was confusing, and we apologize for not making the distinction clearer in the interface.

### "How do I cancel my legacy subscription?"

We've made improvements to the billing interface to make cancellation clearer. Here's how:

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Navigate to **Billing** or **Membership** section
3. Click **Manage Subscription** or **Billing Portal**
4. You can cancel or update your payment method from there

If you're having trouble finding these options, please contact support at [support@softgen.ai](mailto:support@softgen.ai) and we'll help immediately.

### "Am I being charged more now?"

No. If you were on a legacy plan, you continued to be charged at the legacy rate until you actively changed plans or canceled.

The new model is actually **more cost-effective** for most users:
- Annual membership: Only $33
- Pay only for usage (vs paying for unused tokens)
- 15% platform fee (decreasing with community growth)

### "What happened to my unused tokens?"

If you switch from a legacy plan to the new model, we work with you to ensure you don't lose value:
- Remaining tokens are typically converted to wallet credits
- Migration is handled on a case-by-case basis
- Contact support for assistance with your specific situation

## How to Switch to the New Model

If you're still on a legacy plan and want to switch to the new Annual Membership model:

1. **Cancel your legacy subscription** through the billing portal
2. **Join Annual Membership** from the [Membership page](https://softgen.ai/membership)
3. **Add funds to your wallet** to start building
4. **Contact support** if you need help migrating remaining tokens
